2. Information we collect

We collect only the information we need:

  • Enquiry information you give us through our contact form, email, phone or WhatsApp: your name, email address, company, phone number, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • Newsletter information: your email address and your subscription preferences.
  • Client and supplier information needed to deliver or receive services, such as business contact details and correspondence.

We do not intentionally collect special category data through this website. Please do not include sensitive personal information in enquiry messages.
