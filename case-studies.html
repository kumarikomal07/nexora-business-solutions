Case study 01 · Illustrative

Driving Digital Transformation for a Global Enterprise

A multinational business services group modernised its core operations and improved operational efficiency by 40%.

Industry
Professional Services
Region
UK & Europe
Duration
14 months

Client background

A 2,000-person group operating in nine countries, which had grown through acquisitions and was running more than 30 disconnected systems across finance, HR and client delivery.

The challenge

  • Manual re-keying between systems
  • Month-end close taking 15 working days
  • No single view of client profitability

Solution & strategy

We created a value-led digital roadmap, consolidated core platforms in the cloud, automated high-volume workflows and ran a structured adoption programme for 600 users.

40%Improvement in operational efficiency
15 → 6Days to close the month
30 → 9Core systems after consolidation

Key learnings

Early wins in finance built confidence for wider change. Investing in training and champions in each country mattered as much as the technology itself.

Case study 02 · Illustrative

Streamlining Operations for a UK Manufacturer

A family-owned manufacturer reduced lead times and protected margins during a period of rising input costs.

Industry
Manufacturing
Region
United Kingdom
Duration
8 months

Client background

A 250-employee precision components business supplying automotive and aerospace customers from two UK sites.

The challenge

  • Order lead times well above competitors
  • High scrap and rework rates
  • Limited visibility of product-level margins

Solution & strategy

We mapped the end-to-end order process, introduced lean cells and visual management, and built a costing model to show true margin by product and customer.

32%Shorter average order lead time
45%Reduction in scrap and rework
+4 ptsGross margin improvement

Key learnings

Involving shop-floor teams in mapping uncovered the most valuable fixes. Accurate costing changed pricing conversations with two major customers.

Case study 03 · Illustrative

Building a Growth Plan for a Multi-Channel Retailer

A lifestyle retailer refocused its strategy and organisation to grow online sales profitably.

Industry
Retail & E-commerce
Region
UK & Ireland
Duration
6 months

Client background

A retailer with 40 stores and a fast-growing website, whose online growth was not translating into profit.

The challenge

  • Separate store and online teams competing
  • High returns and fulfilment costs
  • No agreed three-year plan

Solution & strategy

We facilitated a new three-year strategy, redesigned the organisation around the customer rather than the channel, and reworked fulfilment and returns economics.

28%Growth in profitable online revenue
18%Lower cost per online order
1Unified customer-centred team

Key learnings

Aligning incentives across channels unlocked collaboration faster than any system change. Clear quarterly targets kept the plan on track.
