1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a comparable way. They can be essential for a site to work, or used to understand how visitors use it.

Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), we ask for your consent before using any cookies or similar technologies that are not strictly necessary.

3. Third-party services

  • Fonts and images are delivered from Google Fonts and Unsplash. These services receive your IP address in order to send the files to your browser, but do not set cookies through our website.
  • WhatsApp links take you to WhatsApp, which applies its own cookie and privacy policies.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you view, block and delete cookies through their settings. Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites work. Guidance is available at ico.org.uk.
