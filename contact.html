Frequently asked questions

What happens after I send an enquiry?

A senior consultant reviews every enquiry and replies within one working day to arrange an initial conversation at a time that suits you.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first 30-minute consultation is free and without obligation. We use it to understand your situation and explain how we could help.

What size of organisations do you work with?

We work mainly with growing small and mid-sized businesses and with larger enterprises on specific programmes. Engagements are scoped to fit your needs and budget.

Do you work outside London and the UK?

Yes. We are headquartered in London and support clients across the UK and internationally, combining on-site work with remote delivery.

How are your consulting fees structured?

Depending on the engagement we offer fixed-fee projects, monthly retainers or day rates. You will receive a clear written proposal before any work begins.

How do you handle confidential information?

We treat all client information as confidential, are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before detailed discussions, and process personal data in line with UK GDPR. See our Privacy Policy.
