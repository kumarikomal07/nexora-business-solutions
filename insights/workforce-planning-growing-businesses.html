HR & Workforce

Workforce planning for growing businesses: getting ahead of the next hire

Nexora Insights16 September 20265 min read

Most growing businesses hire reactively, after the pressure is already showing. A simple workforce plan helps you hire the right roles at the right time.

In many growing businesses, hiring happens when someone is visibly overloaded. By then, quality has often slipped, good people are tired and the new hire will take months to become fully effective. Workforce planning does not need to be complicated to break this cycle.

Link people to the business plan

Start with where the business is going over the next 12 to 24 months: revenue targets, new services, new locations or systems. Then ask what capabilities and capacity that plan requires. This shifts the conversation from "who do we need to replace?" to "what organisation do we need to build?"

Understand today's capacity honestly

Map current roles, responsibilities and workloads. Look for single points of failure, where only one person knows how something works, and for roles that have grown far beyond their original scope. These are usually the first risks to address.

Build, buy or borrow

Not every capability gap needs a permanent hire. Some can be developed internally through training and stretch assignments, some are best recruited and some can be covered with specialist contractors or partners. Making this choice deliberately helps control cost and risk.

Plan for leadership, not just headcount

As teams grow, management spans stretch. Identifying future team leaders early and investing in their development is one of the most effective ways to protect culture and performance through growth.

Review it quarterly

A workforce plan is a living document. Reviewing it alongside financial forecasts each quarter keeps hiring aligned with reality and gives managers clearer expectations about when support is coming.
