2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry and consultation forms: your name, email address, company, phone number, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address and your subscription preferences.
  • WhatsApp, email and phone: your name, contact details and anything you choose to tell us when you contact us.
  • Client engagements: business contact details of client staff and information needed to deliver our services.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics (only with your consent): pages visited, time on site, approximate location (city or country), device and browser type and referring website. See our Cookie Policy.
  • Technical data: your IP address is processed by our hosting provider and by the third-party services that deliver fonts and images to this website, so that content can be sent to your device.
  • Your cookie choices: stored on your device so we can remember them.

We do not intentionally collect special category data (such as health information) through this website. Please do not include it in enquiries.

We only use personal data where we have a lawful basis under UK GDPR:

Responding to enquiries and consultation requestsLegitimate interests (responding to people who contact us) or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract
Delivering consulting services and managing our client relationshipPerformance of a contract; legitimate interests
Sending our newsletterConsent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link
Website analytics to improve our contentConsent, given through our cookie banner

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

6. Your privacy rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure of your data in certain circumstances
  • Restrict how we use your data
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time
  • Data portability for data you provided to us
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent, without affecting earlier processing

To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within one month, which may be extended in limited circumstances. There is normally no fee.

7. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.
